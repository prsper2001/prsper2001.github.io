About

three disciplines, one problem

Human movement doesn't respect departmental boundaries — and neither does my training. I write the code, understand the physiology, and work alongside the clinic: from CUDA kernels and reinforcement learning policies, to gait biomechanics and motion capture, to therapy protocols for stroke and cerebral palsy. My goal is rehabilitation technology that is rigorous as engineering and meaningful as medicine.
